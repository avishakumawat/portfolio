02TestGrid · Website conversion

The problem

People were leaving before they reached the bottom.

Hotjar showed that visitors weren’t getting to the end of key pages. Some sections barely got any engagement, while the forms and CTAs were further down.

25% improvement in demo conversion Across the broader CRO work, not attributed to one individual change.

My approach

I worked with the design and web teams to shorten the pages and make it easier to enquire or book a demo.

  1. 01

    Shorter pages

    I removed sections people weren’t engaging with, tightened the copy and reworked the homepage and hero layouts with more graphics.

  2. 02

    CTAs throughout the page

    I added contextual CTAs and a scroll-triggered prompt, so reaching the bottom wasn’t the only way to take the next step.

  3. 03

    Fewer form fields

    I cut the hero form to four or five qualifying questions, keeping the information sales needed.

  4. 04

    Direct meeting booking

    Some CTAs linked straight to a salesperson’s calendar. Supporting pages also had enquiry forms for visitors with questions.

The live pages have since changed and may not reflect the versions I worked on.

Related pages & wider acquisition results Explore the supporting work

Across the wider work: 30% user growth, ~40% higher paid conversion, and lower ad spend while holding volume and improving lead quality. Channels included Google Ads, LinkedIn, Reddit, Meta, SEO and the website.

03Trustana · AI Commerce Agents GTM

Four buyer questions.
Four clear answers.

GTM strategy, positioning and launch for AI Commerce Agents, a new and technical category for commerce teams.

What shipped

  • Four product pages, one per buyer question
  • Launch video and sales narratives
  • Lifecycle emails, webinar pages, event and partner campaigns

The problem

What does it do? Will it fit? How does it work? Who stays in control?

My decision

Build the story around those four questions. Give governance its own page.

My contribution

GTM, messaging and launch activation. Contract Product & Growth Marketing Manager; partnered with design and web teams.

Closer look

The governance page answers the question that stalls AI purchases: who's in control?

  1. 1The headline names the trade-off directly: real work and real control.
  2. 2The subhead makes control concrete: autonomy, thresholds, approvals and a record of every action.
  3. 3The product UI shows autonomy set per workflow (Review, Assist, Autopilot), proving the claim instead of repeating it.
